## House rules
- Cases are fictional teaching cases. Never use real patient names or real institutions.
- The patient never names a diagnosis or uses medical terms. He describes how things feel.
- Discharge plan points are written for a caregiver with low health literacy: short, concrete, with numbers.
- Every critical fact must be able to "make it home": it needs a conditional plan point and a missed consequence.
